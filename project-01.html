PROJECT 01

Business Launch & Financial Feasibility

Business and financial assessment for the launch of a new professional services business, combining market positioning, pricing, financial feasibility and commercial planning.

Project Type
Business Analysis
Duration
15 Business Days
Project Value
950 € + VAT
Role
Business Consultant / Analyst

The Challenge

The business owner was preparing to launch a boutique professional services company and needed to evaluate the commercial and financial viability of the business before committing capital and creating recurring operating costs.

The project focused on determining the appropriate target customer, service structure, pricing model, required monthly revenue and sales activity needed to operate sustainably.

Scope of Work

The assignment combined commercial analysis and financial modelling to convert the initial business concept into a structured launch decision.

Market Analysis Market structure, customer needs and competitive environment.
Competitor Analysis Comparison of competing offers, pricing and market positioning.
Offer & Pricing Service structure, minimum project fee and commercial pricing.
Financial Feasibility Revenue, costs, break-even and 12-month financial model.
Commercial KPIs Lead flow, conversion, average project value and utilization.
Launch Roadmap 90-day action plan with measurable decision checkpoints.

Commercial Findings

The analysis identified a viable market position between low-cost freelance services and higher-priced boutique consulting. The final service model was structured around clearly defined projects with fixed scope, measurable outputs and transparent pricing.

The commercial model produced 22 qualified leads per month, a 41% lead-to-proposal conversion rate and a 44% proposal-to-client conversion rate. Average project value reached 720 €, while referral and repeat revenue represented 24% of the revenue mix.

Financial Assessment

Under the Base Scenario, first-year revenue reached 34,560 €. With fixed operating costs of 1,050 € per month and variable costs equal to 12% of revenue, the model generated an operating result of 17,813 € before taxes, social security obligations and owner compensation.

Monthly operating break-even was calculated at 1,193 €, while a management safety threshold of 1,700 € was established to provide additional operating flexibility.

Final Decision

GO

The launch was assessed as financially viable under a lean operating structure, without permanent staffing at the initial stage and with a maximum setup budget of 2,400 €.

The first 90 days were defined as a validation period, with pricing, conversion, average project value and monthly revenue used as the primary decision points before increasing fixed costs or expanding capacity.
