Market Positioning & Industry Intelligence

Market and competitor intelligence project designed to identify commercial opportunities, prioritize customer segments and define a stronger market position for a B2B professional services company.

The Challenge

The company had an established presence in the market, but its commercial positioning had developed without a clear strategic structure.

Management needed to understand where the company stood against competing providers, which customer segments offered the strongest commercial opportunity and how its services should be positioned and priced.

Scope of Work

The project combined market intelligence, competitor analysis, customer segment evaluation and pricing research to create a decision-ready positioning framework.

Market Intelligence Buying criteria, market signals, demand drivers and commercial barriers.
Competitor Intelligence Six competitor profiles covering price, specialization, offers and positioning.
Segment Prioritization Assessment of need, budget, repeat potential, accessibility and sales friction.
Positioning Strategy Definition of the strongest defendable position within the competitive landscape.
Pricing Intelligence Offer architecture and pricing levels aligned with the selected position.
Commercial Roadmap 60-day implementation plan with measurable commercial checkpoints.

Market Findings

The analysis identified a competitive gap between low-cost execution providers and high-end advisory firms.

Buyers placed the greatest value on clear scope, transparent pricing, specialist expertise, speed of delivery and evidence that the work would support a specific business decision.

This created an opportunity to compete on structured specialist delivery rather than on price alone.

Priority Segment

Four customer segments were evaluated against commercial need, budget capacity, repeat potential, accessibility and sales friction.

SMEs with 10-50 employees achieved the highest overall attractiveness score at 86/100 and were selected as the primary commercial target.

Scale-ups represented a secondary opportunity, while micro businesses showed greater price sensitivity and enterprise accounts required longer procurement and sales cycles.

Positioning Decision

REPOSITION

The recommended position was defined as a specialized mid-premium partner: positioned above generalist execution providers while remaining more flexible and accessible than traditional premium consulting firms.

The commercial proposition was redesigned around clearly scoped, decision-oriented projects rather than a broad catalogue of general services.

Offer Architecture

The final pricing structure established four clear commercial entry points: a 420 € Business Diagnostic, a 980 € Core Project, a 1,450 € Growth Sprint and a 480 € monthly Management Retainer.

The Core Project became the main pricing anchor, supporting the new mid-premium position while maintaining a clear distinction from both low-cost providers and premium advisory firms.

Business Value

The project converted a broad and inconsistent commercial proposition into a structured market position with a defined target customer, clearer pricing and measurable commercial priorities.

A 60-day implementation plan was established to validate the new positioning through qualified conversations, proposals, conversion rate and realized average project value before further price increases or expansion.
