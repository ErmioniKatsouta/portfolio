BE Aligned - Ordering & Ticketing Platform

A professional digital platform combining structured ordering, support ticketing and custom software integration within a unified operational environment.

The Challenge

The project required a professional digital environment capable of bringing key operational processes together rather than handling them through disconnected workflows.

Ordering, support requests and custom functionality needed to operate as parts of the same platform, creating a more structured experience for professional users and a clearer operational process behind the service.

Solution Framework

The platform was structured around a professional portal connecting ordering, support ticketing and custom software functionality.

Professional Portal Central digital environment for the core functions associated with the service.
Ordering Structured workflow supporting the creation and management of orders.
Ticketing Organized process for submitting and managing support requests.
Custom Integration Custom functionality connecting the platform with the operational requirements of the project.

02

03

04

05
Closed The support case is completed and closed.

Functional Analysis

The project was approached from the perspective of functional requirements and digital workflows: what users need to do, what information is required at each stage and how the different platform components interact.

Particular attention was given to the relationship between ordering and support. Instead of treating support as a completely separate process, ticketing becomes part of the broader operating environment surrounding the platform.

Business Value

FROM DISCONNECTED OPERATIONAL TASKS TO A STRUCTURED DIGITAL WORKFLOW.

The project demonstrates how ordering, support and custom functionality can be brought together within a single professional platform rather than operating as separate digital processes.

Its value lies in the connection between business requirements and digital delivery: translating operational needs into a structured platform experience.
