PROJECT 03

Business Plan & Investment Readiness

Business planning and financial assessment project designed to evaluate growth potential, capital requirements and investment readiness through a structured three-year operating and financial plan.

Project Type
Business Planning
Duration
20 Business Days
Project Value
1,350 € + VAT
Role
Business Consultant / Analyst

The Challenge

The project required the development of an integrated business plan capable of supporting both an internal investment decision and future discussions with financing or investment partners.

The central question was not simply whether the business could grow, but whether the proposed operating model could generate sufficient revenue, margin and cash capacity to justify the required capital commitment.

Scope of Work

The project combined strategic planning, commercial assumptions, operational requirements and financial modelling into one decision framework.

Business Model Customer groups, value proposition, services and revenue logic.
Market Assessment Demand, customer segments, competition and market positioning.
Commercial Plan Pricing, acquisition channels, pipeline and conversion assumptions.
Operating Model Capacity, staffing requirements and fixed and variable cost structure.
Financial Forecast Three-year revenue, gross profit, operating expenses and EBITDA model.
Investment Readiness Funding requirement, use of funds, scenarios, risks and investment milestones.

Business Model

The final model focused on B2B customers with recurring needs and clearly scoped projects.

Revenue was structured across core projects, higher-value implementation engagements and recurring support, reducing dependence on a single type of commercial activity.

Growth was designed to come primarily from improved utilization and average project value before materially increasing the permanent cost base.

Financial Outlook

The base case projected revenue of 168,000 € in Year 1, 228,000 € in Year 2 and 294,000 € in Year 3.

EBITDA was projected to increase from 22,500 € in Year 1 to 44,800 € in Year 2 and 70,400 € in Year 3 as fixed costs were absorbed across a larger revenue base.

The corresponding EBITDA margin improved from 13.4% to 19.6% and finally 23.9% in Year 3.

Funding Structure

The initial funding requirement was assessed at 75,000 €. The recommended structure combined founder capital, equipment finance and a working-capital facility.

Capital was allocated across equipment and systems, working-capital reserves, commercial development and a contingency buffer.

The funding plan deliberately protected liquidity rather than directing the entire investment toward immediate expansion.

Scenario Analysis

Three scenarios were assessed to test the resilience of the business model.

The conservative scenario produced 132,000 € in Year 1 revenue and 6,800 € EBITDA. The base scenario produced 168,000 € in revenue and 22,500 € EBITDA, while the growth scenario reached 205,000 € in revenue and 38,900 € EBITDA.

The analysis showed that the business could remain operationally positive under slower growth, although cash reserves became increasingly important in the conservative case.

Investment Readiness

The project concluded that the business was investment-ready with conditions.

Before the second phase of capital deployment, management should confirm a rolling 90-day pipeline of at least 45,000 €, gross margin above 55%, cash runway of at least four months and proposal conversion of at least 30%.

These checkpoints transformed the investment decision from a one-time commitment into a measurable staged process.

Final Decision

PROCEED WITH STAGED INVESTMENT

The financial model supported the growth plan, but the main risk remained the speed at which the commercial pipeline could be converted into realized revenue.

A staged investment approach was therefore recommended, with additional capital deployment linked directly to commercial performance, margin and cash-runway thresholds.
